Black Hole

A successful actress's career comes to a sudden stop when she reaches 40 and her agent tells her that she is now in the Black Hole of Age. Haunted by that comment, we follow her one afternoon as she spirals into the horrors and amusements of ageism. What is perception and what is imagination? How do people see her — if they see her at all?

Genre
Psychological comedy / surreal horror
Format
Short film
Writer / Director
Pia Mechler

About Pia Mechler

Born in Germany to a German father and a Polish/Chilean mother, Pia Mechler studied film at the acclaimed European Film College in Denmark. She is an award-nominated director (Almost Settled, Everything Is Wonderful, Black Hole) and actor (Devils (Sky), The Upside, American Sausage Standoff and many more). She resides in Brooklyn, New York.
